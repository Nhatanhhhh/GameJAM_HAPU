========================================
LIES UNDER THE LEAF
========================================

## ABOUT THE GAME

"Lies Under the Leaf" is a short puzzle adventure game.

Your goal is to **guide the player** through a forest of strange leaves,
and reach the **Lotus Flower** to complete each level.

Each type of leaf behaves differently.
Observe carefully, and plan your path to move forward.

## HOW TO PLAY

- Double click on the file [Lies Under the Leaf.exe] inside the [Lies Under the Leaf] folder to open the game.

- Move Left: [A]
- Move Right: [D]
- Jump: [SPACE]
- Grab / Drag/ Hover: [Left Mouse Button]

Use your mouse to drag and interact with objects.
Watch how leaves react to your mouse — each one hides a secret.

## LEAF TYPES

There are **7 types of leaves** in the game:

1. **Normal Leaf** – A solid platform for basic movement.
2. **Free Move Leaf** – Can be grabbed and dragged freely in any direction.
3. **Vertical Leaf** – Can only move up and down.
4. **Horizontal Leaf** – Can only move left and right.
5. **Phantom Leaf** – Invisible until hovered with the mouse.
6. **SuperPhantom Leaf** – Turns a nearby leaf into a Phantom.
7. **Coil Leaf** – Launches the player upward.

## OBJECTIVE

Find the correct path using the different leaves.
Reach the Lotus Flower to clear the level.

## GAME STRUCTURE

1. **Tutorial / Mission Briefing**
   Learn how the leaves behave and what your goal is.

2. **Gameplay**
   Control your character, solve the puzzle, and reach the Lotus.

3. **Ending / Credits**
   Watch the final sequence and see the full story unfold.
